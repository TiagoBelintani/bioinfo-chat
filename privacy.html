Privacy Notice

BioInfo Chat · Bioinformatics, Omics & Evolution

Overview

BioInfo Chat is an experimental academic tool developed for educational and scientific-support purposes. This notice explains how information may be processed when the service is used.

Information submitted to the chat

Questions, prompts and other text submitted through BioInfo Chat are transmitted to the application's backend and processed using third-party artificial intelligence infrastructure provided through GroqCloud.

Users should not submit confidential, unpublished, sensitive or personally identifiable information unless they are authorized to do so and understand the implications of transmitting such information to third-party services.

Third-party processing

BioInfo Chat currently uses GroqCloud to process conversational requests.

Data handling by Groq is governed by Groq's own terms, privacy practices and data-control policies. Those policies may change independently of BioInfo Chat.

Conversation history

When conversation history is enabled, BioInfo Chat stores recent conversations locally in the user's browser using localStorage.

This locally stored history is not designed to synchronize across devices and can be removed by clearing the history inside the application or by clearing browser storage.

Technical data

Hosting, infrastructure and API providers may process technical information necessary to operate the service, such as request timestamps, IP-related network information, error information, browser information and service-usage metadata.

Data minimization

BioInfo Chat is intended for academic assistance rather than storage of research datasets or personal records. Users should submit only the information necessary for the particular academic question being asked.

External services

BioInfo Chat may rely on external infrastructure, including Vercel for application hosting and GroqCloud for AI inference. These providers operate under their respective terms and privacy policies.

Contact

Questions concerning BioInfo Chat can be directed to the project creator through the contact information available on the academic website.
